B777 Tools — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Summary

B777 Tools does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does with data

  • Everything except the Live tab works offline. The Live tab downloads public aviation data (NAT tracks from the FAA; METAR, TAF, SIGMET, AIREP and space weather from NOAA). These requests carry no personal data; like any internet request, the servers see the address of the device.
  • The last copy of the downloaded data is kept on the device so it can be read without a connection.
  • ATC to Text uses the microphone only while you press LISTEN, and turns speech into text with the device's own speech recognition. On devices that support it, this happens entirely on the device; on older devices iOS may send the audio to Apple to recognise it, under Apple's privacy policy. Nothing is recorded, and the text is kept only while the page is open.
  • It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software.
  • The numbers and reports you type are used on your device to show a result. They are not saved and they never leave the device.
  • The app remembers two preferences on your device: the text size and the converter page you used last.
  • Pasting a report uses the system Paste button, so the app only receives the clipboard when you tap it.

Children

The app is a reference tool for airline pilots and collects no data from anyone.
