B777 Tools — Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Summary

B777 Tools does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does with data

  • The app works entirely offline. It makes no network connections.
  • It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software.
  • The numbers and reports you type are used on your device to show a result. They are not saved and they never leave the device.
  • The app opens with an invitation code. The code is checked on your device and kept in its keychain; it is not sent anywhere.
  • The app remembers two preferences on your device: the text size and the converter page you used last.
  • Pasting a report uses the system Paste button, so the app only receives the clipboard when you tap it.

Children

The app is a reference tool for airline pilots and collects no data from anyone.
