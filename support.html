B777 Tools — Support

B777 Tools is a study aid for 777-300ER freighter crews: calculators, memory item drills, study notes, an interactive FMA, airport briefings, a weather and NOTAM decoder, a NAT HLA guide and live NAT tracks and weather.

Study aid only. It is not approved documentation and it is not for operational use. The approved manuals, charts, NOTAMs and company instructions always take precedence.

Found an error?

Please write with the page, the value shown and the document and revision that says otherwise. The revisions the app was built from are listed under Study → About & Sources.

Questions

  • How do I get the app? Through the App Store link shared by the person who told you about it. There is no account and no registration.
  • The text is too small. Tap the "AA" button at the top left and choose Large or Extra Large.
  • Does it need a connection? Only the Live tab, to download NAT tracks and weather. Everything else is on the device, and Live keeps the last copy for use offline.
  • Does it collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
  • Which devices? iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.
